Project goals

Three goals.One build.

01★
Stay close to the original
Use parts backed by official evidence, so the mechanics, electronics, and software stay as close to the original design as we can get them.
02★
Keep the process open
Share sourcing, manufacturing, assembly, tests — and the failures — wherever licensing allows, so anyone can trace and repeat the work.
03★
Make it fun to live with
Turn the build into a real companion — new moves, games, and voice tricks, shared the moment they work on the robot.
